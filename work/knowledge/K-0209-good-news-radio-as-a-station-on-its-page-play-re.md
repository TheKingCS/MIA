Zac: the radio was nothing but a card, usable only through chat. The page (web/news.js) is now the station. What it shows is assembled in core/good_news.screen(): the episode's stories with summary, how each is known, caveats and where to read it in full; every personality's script and recording; the stories followed with what was reported since; the outlets read. What it changes goes through actions in core/news_actions.py (news.turn_on, news.turn_off, news.make, news.follow, news.unfollow, news.misleading), each with its voice phrase in good_news.reply(), so nothing is page-only (DEC-0125). Following: follow() keeps the story's title, topic and links with the words that identify it (_keywords, plurals stemmed); each day's reading calls keep_updates(context, items) on every item the feeds returned, so a later report is kept even when it isn't good news, and is tagged as an update. Playing: a recording is fetched from the music library when the episode has one; otherwise her own voice reads the script a part at a time (/api/heavy/speak cuts at 2000 characters, so the script is split on blank lines and the next part is fetched while this one plays). POST /api/news/heard counts a play once a day (note_listened), so stories heard on the page count toward the misleading share like ones asked for. Home's card and the glasses panel show an episode only while it is fresh. What went wrong: (1) node.replaceChildren(null) prints the word null; filter falsy children first (the page's fill()). (2) MIAShell.el flattens children one level only, so a map returning arrays inside an array printed commas and raw links; use flatMap. (3) The audio element's paused flag is true between two parts read aloud and while a part is being fetched, so a button drawn from it flickers and a pause is lost; the page keeps its own `wanted` (the station is meant to be playing) and `ours` (a pause the page made itself, so a headset's pause button still counts as the person's). (4) A ticket number, raised on every stop or new play, lets a late answer from the voice be dropped instead of starting to play over another voice. (5) In a test, loops._since must be pinned or the 30-day window moves past the fixture's dates. Checked with tools.page_check and a headless Edge drive with a stand-in voice, not a unit test: no test starts a browser.
