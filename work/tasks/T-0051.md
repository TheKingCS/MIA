Opened by the loop `phone.vulnerable` (docs/IMPROVEMENT_LOOPS.md, DEC-0094): packages the phone app bundles with a known vulnerability 3 is above its floor of 0.

Measured: 3 of 18 phone packages with a known vulnerability: cryptography 42.0.8 (11), fastapi 0.99.1 (1), starlette 0.27.0 (14) (from 18).

Find why and fix it through the lab; the loop measures again each day and reopens this if it stays there.

- 2026-11-06 claude: Why they are held back: android-phone/app/build.gradle.kts pins fastapi<0.100 and pydantic<2 (pydantic 2 needs pydantic-core, a native wheel Chaquopy must provide for Android), and fastapi 0.99.1 allows only starlette 0.27.x; cryptography comes from Chaquopy own wheels. The phone server listens on 127.0.0.1 only, which limits who can reach it. The fix is a phone-build change: find which pydantic-core and cryptography wheels Chaquopy 17 offers for arm64, move the phone to fastapi and pydantic 2 (the PC already runs them), build, and try it on the phone. Needs a real phone test before it ships.

- 2026-11-06 claude: Checked Chaquopy own index (chaquo.com/pypi-13.1): cryptography stops at 42.0.8 for Android, and there is no pydantic-core, so neither can be upgraded from its wheels. Options for Zac: (a) keep them, with the exposure written down: the phone server answers only on 127.0.0.1, so only apps on the phone can reach it; (b) build newer cryptography for Android ourselves (a Rust cross-compile, then keep it current); (c) take cryptography off the phone where it is used (core/password_vault.py, core/private_journal.py, core/secrets_manager.py, core/web_push.py) by using Android Keystore through Kotlin, and replace fastapi/starlette on the phone with a smaller server. Claude recommends (a) now and (c) over time.
