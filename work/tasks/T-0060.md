Zac, 2026-11-22: "making MIA super capable for teaching homestead and construction thoroughly step by step
offline. She is to be the ultimate handy tool and guide for all self sufficient and homestead related actions. I
want to be able to have auto generated missions for plans we confirm together for different builds or gardens,
projects, etc"

**What it means.** Any build, garden or homestead project (a shed, a raised bed, a chicken coop, a greenhouse bed,
a root cellar, canning, fencing...) is planned with him, confirmed by him, and then becomes a project with its
missions made from the plan, each worked step by step on its own screen and by voice, all of it offline.

**What's already there (build on it, never a parallel system):**
- Plans with worked-out materials and cuts: `core/plan_library.py`, quantities in `core/build_calcs.py` (DEC-0118;
  the model never does the arithmetic).
- Projects and missions joined: `core/mission_projects.py` (DEC-0152); guided steps: `core/mission_guide.py`
  `GUIDES` (trade, greenhouse). A new kind of guided mission is one more guide there.
- Step-by-step teaching with stages and verifying: learning paths (`core/paths.py`, DEC-0112), Trade Master's
  sessions (`core/trade_session.py`), Step By Step plans (`core/step_plans.py`).
- Specialists for construction and homesteading (`docs/specialists/`, another session's work in progress: ask
  before touching it).
- Guided steps live in Missions and their own page, never on Today or Home (Zac, 2026-11-09).

**To build (a plan first, for Zac to confirm):**
1. Plan together: a conversation and a screen that turn "I want a 4x8 raised bed" or "a coop for six hens" into a
   draft plan (from a built-in calculator, a kept plan, or a new one drafted with him), shown with materials, tools,
   stages and steps, changed with him until he presses Confirm. Nothing is made before that press.
2. Missions from the confirmed plan: one project, one mission per stage (site, materials, build, finish...), each
   step with why, what to watch for and a checkpoint (the `mission_guide` shape), the shopping list from his
   inventory; dates only where he gives them. Made by one action with one Undo.
3. Teaching depth offline: each step's how-to from local sources (the plan's own steps, the specialists, offline
   reference such as Kiwix ZIMs per the roadmap), safety and code notes from code (build_calcs' code-and-span-table
   note), never invented by the model.
4. Gardens and homestead skills too: beds and plantings (with Homestead's own data), preserving, animals; each a
   plan kind with its calculator or template.
5. Both ways on every device (DEC-0125): a tool and a screen control for each new action, tiers in the manifest
   (DEC-0156), a loop or a reason (DEC-0094), docs.

Questions for Zac before building: which first (a build, a garden, or both together)? Which offline references to
fetch (Kiwix ZIMs, his own PDFs in the Library)?
