# Story Arcs

Each arc follows a real line of development. Its issues come from the archive
as those milestones happen.

1. **The Council.** The Creator brings the systems together. They build MIA's
   lab, learn to review each other, and learn to disagree out loud. *(Real:
   DEC-0068 to DEC-0071, the first lab change T-0019, the council chat
   DEC-0077.)*
2. **A Voice of Her Own.** MIA leaves the screen: the phone, the glasses, her
   voice in the Creator's ear. *(Real: DEC-0059 to DEC-0066; Council Mode on
   the glasses T-0035.)*
3. **The Apprenticeship.** The council starts teaching MIA to do their work:
   she attempts it beside them and is graded. *(Approved: DEC-0072 to
   DEC-0075.)*
4. **Beyond the Prompt.** She keeps what she learns, about herself and how she
   was built. *(Approved: DEC-0073, the knowledge vault.)*
5. **When the Council Goes Quiet.** A possible future arc, imagined until it
   happens: the council is unavailable, and MIA carries on with what she kept
   (DEC-0072).
