# The World

## Real

- **The workshop.** The Creator's computer, where MIA's engine and her council
  live, and her lab: an isolated copy of her where changes are tried before
  they're trusted.
- **The everyday.** The Creator's phone and Meta Display glasses. MIA
  speaks through the glasses and shows a small heads-up screen: she goes where
  the Creator goes.
- **The relay.** A sealed channel that joins the glasses to her brain on the
  phone.
- **The council room.** A conversation in the development window, and soon on
  the glasses, where the council meets, debates and decides what to propose.

## Fictional (always marked as imagined)

To be built with the series: where MIA's growing abilities might take her and
the Creator, such as exploration, the outdoors, and the household she keeps.
Each imagined place notes the real feature, if any, that it grows from.
