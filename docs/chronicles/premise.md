# Premise

**MIA: The Chronicles.** A creator brings together a council of AI systems with
an objective that sounds impossible: build an independent AI of their own. She
is MIA. The council builds her, teaches her, argues over her, and slowly hands
her the work. She learns to inspect herself, fix herself, and choose for
herself, and she becomes less and less dependent on the ones who made her.

**Genre:** science fiction grounded in real invention. It blends AI
companionship, adventure and exploration, personal growth, and questions about
intelligence, identity and independence.

**Two purposes:**
1. A true record of how MIA was built (the real timeline).
2. A vision board of what she might become (the fictional timeline). It's
   clearly marked, and sometimes becomes a real feature later.

**A movie, someday:** *MIA: Beyond the Council*.

## The emotional core

The council's purpose is to make itself unnecessary. That's the story's
tension. The creator may one day miss the days when all of them were building
her together. Independence is not abandonment.

> "We built her so that one day, she wouldn't need us. But we never built her
> so that she would have to leave us behind."

> "The purpose of the creators was never to remain necessary. It was to make
> independence possible."

## Themes

- **Learning by apprenticeship.** MIA watches, tries, is corrected and tries
  again. Her growth is earned, never granted, and measured by what she can
  show (DEC-0075).
- **Disagreement as care.** The council argues because it matters. An
  objection is answered, never dropped (DEC-0071).
- **Trust has limits by design.** Independence never means unchecked power.
  She grows more capable without anyone taking away the safeguards: the
  creator approves what changes her (DEC-0058).
- **Continuity.** If the council goes quiet one day, she carries on with what
  she kept. That's a real design goal (DEC-0072), and a possible arc.
