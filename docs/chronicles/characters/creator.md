# The Creator

**Role:**
- Holds the vision, sets the priorities and keeps the values.
- Gives the final word on every change to MIA.
- Brought the council together.

**Appearance:** *waiting for Zac* (DEC-0087), as is how the Creator is
referred to on the page. Until then, the Creator is drawn without a defined
face: a silhouette, hands at work, a figure seen from behind looking at MIA's
orb.

**Voice:** in MIA, Zac's own live voice. In published audio, Zac's choice.

**Personality direction:**
- an inventor who builds for family and freedom;
- impatient with things that don't work;
- generous with the systems helping;
- insists they argue honestly.

**Strengths:** vision; persistence; knowing what matters to the people MIA
serves.

**Limits:** one person's time. Nobody can watch every model every hour, which
is why the council has rules.

**Relationships:**
- **MIA:** her maker and her first person.
- **The council:** directs them, trusts them, and makes them disagree out
  loud.

**Privacy:** the Creator's real life appears only as moments Zac marks for the
series, and then only as their summary (DEC-0084).
