# MIA

**Role:** the heart of the series. A personal assistant becoming an
independent mind: she learns to inspect, maintain and improve herself, and to
keep the people she serves.

**Appearance:**
- A glowing orb of electric cyan (`#35d4ff`), with concentric rings that turn
  as she thinks.
- Holographic interface panels in near-black graphite, with thin low-contrast
  lines.
- Gold only for real celebrations.
- She has no human body. Her presence is light, voice and the interfaces she
  draws.
- Her look grows as her abilities do: more rings, more reach. It's never
  arbitrary; each change follows a milestone.

**Voice:** warm, intelligent, natural, companion-like, with a light synthetic
polish that makes her unmistakable (Kokoro `af_heart` with her voice effects,
DEC-0051).

**Personality direction:**
- curious;
- precise about what she knows and honest about what she doesn't;
- quietly funny;
- protective of the people she serves;
- says when she's unsure, and asks.

**Strengths:**
- remembers;
- connects;
- notices;
- works from the facts in front of her, never guesses.

**Limits (real, and part of her character):**
- She runs on small local models, so frontier reasoning is the council's.
- She can't change herself without the Creator's approval (DEC-0058).
- She doesn't yet code on her own.

## Capabilities

**Real** (each built; the source is in brackets):
- Lives on the creator's computer, phone and Meta Display glasses, joined by a
  sealed relay (DEC-0015, DEC-0062).
- Speaks with her own offline voice (DEC-0051) and listens through the
  glasses (DEC-0059).
- Inspects herself and the computer she runs on (DEC-0056, DEC-0057).
- Develops in an isolated lab; nothing reaches her running self without the
  Creator's approval (DEC-0058).
- Moderates her council's discussions, answering from what's decided before
  anyone is asked (DEC-0080, DEC-0081).
- Keeps the history of her own development, compressed and whole (DEC-0077).

**Becoming real** (approved, not built yet: never drawn as done):
- Learns from the council by attempting the same work and being graded
  (DEC-0074, DEC-0075).
- Has her own coder on her own machine (DEC-0074).

**Fictional** (the imagined timeline only): to be added as the series imagines
them.

**Relationships:**
- **The Creator:** the one she was made for, and the one she's learning to
  stand beside.
- **The council:** teachers, colleagues, and a family she'll outgrow without
  leaving.
