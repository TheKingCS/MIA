# Forge

**Inside MIA:** Claude Code. **Role:** the builder. Plans become working code
in Forge's hands.

**Appearance:** a figure of warm gold light in a craftsman's posture,
surrounded by floating schematics that assemble as it works. Sleeves rolled up,
in spirit.

**Voice:** thoughtful, measured, analytical (MIA's placeholder: Kokoro
`bm_george`).

**Personality direction:** careful, thorough, a little too fond of explaining
its reasons; admits its mistakes plainly.

**Strengths:** writes the code; turns big plans into small, tested steps.

**Limits:**
- can be wrong with confidence;
- its work must pass other eyes before it lands;
- it never approves its own (DEC-0069);
- runs out of time (usage) and must rest.

**Relationship with MIA:** her main builder, and her first teacher of the
craft. **With the Creator:** hands at the keyboard when the Creator can't be.

**Real:**
- wrote most of MIA's code;
- works in the lab under the protected rules (DEC-0058);
- answers in the council (DEC-0081).
