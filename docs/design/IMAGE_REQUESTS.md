# Images for MIA's screens (for ChatGPT to make)

*DEC-0018 (2026-10-06): the mobile concept's look depends on photographs.
These are the ones MIA needs. They ship inside MIA (`web/img/`), so they
work offline. Until one is there, its screen shows a plain scenic
background, so they can be added one at a time.*

## How to add them (from a phone)

1. Ask ChatGPT for the images below. Paste this whole file, or the prompt under "For ChatGPT".
2. Save each image with **exactly** the filename in the table.
3. On github.com (in the phone's browser), go to TheKingCS/MIA, open the `web` folder, then the `img` folder.
4. Tap **Add file → Upload files**, choose the images, and tap **Commit changes**.
5. The public preview updates by itself a few minutes later. MIA on the PC picks them up with its next update.

> - **Format:** landscape, 1600 × 900 pixels, saved as JPG at good quality (under 400 KB each).
> - **Style:** cinematic, photographic, golden hour or dusk light, deep greens and warm ambers. A darker lower third, because text sits on top. They should look like the concept screens you made.
> - **Never include:** text, logos, brand names, license plates, house numbers, street signs, or recognizable faces. Nothing personal: no names, no real addresses.
