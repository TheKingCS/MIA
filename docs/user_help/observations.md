# Observations: what MIA has noticed

## What shows up here

Observations is a read-only list of things MIA has noticed across your
other modules that still need attention — right now that's overdue or
due-soon Maintenance tasks, and Missions that have gone quiet for two
weeks or more. Each one is grouped under its own section (Maintenance,
Missions) and shows what was noticed plus a suggested next step.

## Nothing to add or edit here

You can't create or dismiss an entry directly in this screen. An
observation clears itself automatically the moment you act on it in
its own module — completing the overdue Maintenance task, or making
real progress on (or abandoning) the stale Mission. Observations is
just a window into that same real state, not a second copy of it.

## Also on the Home dashboard and in chat

A small "Observations" card on the Home dashboard shows how many
things are currently open, and updates the "welcome back" briefing
too. You can also just ask MIA directly — "what have you noticed?" or
"any observations?" — instead of opening this module at all.
