# People & Pets: relationship and pet profiles

## What this module tracks

People & Pets has two tabs: People (the people in your life) and Pets
(your animals). Each entry keeps a running note of the things you'd
otherwise have to remember on your own.

## Adding a person

On the People tab, click Add Person and fill in their name, your
relationship to them, and — optionally — their birthday, favorite
things, gift ideas, and any notes (shared memories, anything else
worth remembering). Edit Selected and Delete Selected manage existing
entries. A tracked birthday shows a "birthday in N days" tag right in
the list.

## Adding a pet

The Pets tab works the same way — Add Pet takes a name, species, an
optional birthday or adoption date, medical notes (allergies, vet
visit history — whatever you'd want on hand), and general notes.

## Birthdays and gift reminders

Once a person's birthday is set, MIA can remind you about it ahead of
time — 7 days out, by default, so there's real time to actually get a
gift. If you've saved gift ideas for them, the reminder includes those
too. The Home dashboard's People & Pets card always shows whoever's
birthday is coming up soonest.
