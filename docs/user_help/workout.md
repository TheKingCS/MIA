# Workout: exercises, templates, guided sessions, and progress

## What the Workout module tracks

Workout has five tabs: Exercises (your exercise library), Templates
(named workouts made of exercises), Log Session (where you actually
work out), History (past sessions), and Progress (personal records and
a weight-over-time chart per exercise).

## Adding an exercise

On the Exercises tab, click Add Exercise and give it a name, a
category (like Legs, Back, or Chest), and optionally the equipment it
uses. Edit Selected or Delete Selected work on whichever exercise is
highlighted in the list.

## Building a template

A Template is a named workout made of exercises — think "Push Day" or
"Leg Day." On the Templates tab, click Add Template to name it, then
select it and use Add Exercise… to build its exercise list one at a
time; Remove Selected takes one back out.

## Starting a guided session

Log Session is where a real workout happens. Click Start Session and
pick a template — this starts a real stopwatch tracking your total
session time. Pick your current exercise from the dropdown (you can
switch exercises anytime, not just in a fixed order — real workouts
revisit exercises and superset) and click Log Set to record a weight
and rep count for it. Start Resting begins an independent rest timer
between sets. Nothing is saved until you click Finish Session — up
until then it's all just tracked live on screen.

## History and progress

The History tab lists every finished session with its date, template,
duration, and set count. The Progress tab shows your personal record
for each exercise (the heaviest weight you've ever logged for it, not
an estimated max) plus a chart of that weight over time.
