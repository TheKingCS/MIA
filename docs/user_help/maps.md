# Maps: Waypoint Plots, a Real Offline Basemap, and Trail Maps

## What the Maps module does

The Maps module has three parts, each showing a different kind of map:
a schematic plot of your own Waypoints, a real pannable/zoomable
offline basemap, and a catalog of official state park and trail map
PDFs.

## Waypoints tab: distance and bearing

This is a schematic plot of your saved Waypoints — click one to set it
as your starting point, then click another to see the real distance
and compass bearing between them. Pick a Trip from the route dropdown
to overlay its planned route as a line connecting its Waypoints in
order. This tab is deliberately not a real map — it's a quick, offline
distance/bearing tool, same data as the Navigation module's Waypoint
list.

## Basemap tab: a real offline map

This is a genuine pannable, zoomable map — drag to pan, scroll to
zoom, just like any map app. There's a source switcher: USGS Topo
shows real US topographic detail (contour lines, real trails), while
OpenStreetMap gives a worldwide low-zoom overview for areas outside
the US.

To make an area work fully offline (no internet needed once cached),
use one of the download buttons: "Download Kentucky" and "Download
Tennessee" cache those states at real trail-navigation detail,
"Download Current View" caches whatever area you're currently looking
at (pan/zoom anywhere first), and "Download Worldwide Overview" caches
a small low-zoom view of the entire planet. Live panning fetches
individual tiles on demand too, if you have a connection.

## Trail Maps tab: official park PDFs

A catalog of real official trail map PDFs — add one by pasting a URL
to a park's own published map, or by browsing to a PDF file you
already have. Double-click (or select and press View) to open it right
in an embedded viewer — scroll down if the map itself is on a later
page than the cover. Delete removes a catalog entry and its file.
