# Kitchen: recipes, pantry, grocery list, and meal tracking

## What the Kitchen module tracks

Kitchen has five tabs: Recipes, Pantry, Grocery List, Meal Log, and
Suggestions (recipes you can actually make right now with what's on
hand).

## Adding a recipe

On the Recipes tab, click Add Recipe and give it a name and category.
Select it and click View Details to open its own page, where Add
Ingredient… builds its ingredient list one item at a time (Remove
Selected takes one back out) and you can fill in freeform
instructions.

## Tracking your pantry

The Pantry tab is your actual on-hand inventory — Add Pantry Item logs
a name, quantity, unit, and optional expiration date; Edit Selected
and Delete Selected manage existing entries. Suggestions compares your
Pantry against every Recipe's ingredient list and shows what you can
make right now, and what's still missing for the rest.

## Logging a meal

From a recipe's own detail page, "Log that I made this today" records
it in one click. You can also log a meal directly from the Meal Log
tab's own Log a Meal button. Either way it updates that recipe's
last-made date and how many times you've made it.

## Grocery list

Add Item on the Grocery List tab adds something by hand; "Add Missing
Ingredients to Grocery List" (on a recipe's detail page) adds whatever
that recipe needs that your Pantry doesn't already have, in one click.
Check items off as you shop, then Clear Checked Items to tidy up.
