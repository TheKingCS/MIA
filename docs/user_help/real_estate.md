# Real Estate: property portfolio and rental tracking

## Adding a property

Click Add Property and fill in its details — address, type (Rental,
Investment, Primary Residence, etc.), current value, and mortgage
info. Edit Selected changes it later; View Details opens its own full
page.

## A property's detail page

A property's detail page shows its equity, NOI, and Cap Rate for
whichever date range you pick (This Month/This Year/All Time). Record
Rental Income and Record Expense log real transactions against that
specific property — these flow straight into the Budget module's own
Income/Expenses, tagged to this property automatically. Link
Maintenance Asset… connects it to a real asset in the Maintenance
module (so its upkeep history shows here too); Unlink Maintenance
Asset removes that connection without deleting either record.

## Depreciation and mortgage payoff

For an eligible Rental or Investment property with a land value and
placed-in-service date entered, real straight-line depreciation is
computed automatically. With loan terms entered (rate, term, start
date), a real amortization schedule projects your remaining balance
and payoff date, and correctly splits interest from principal for tax
reporting — see Budget's own Business Report export for the full
Schedule E and 1099 paperwork this feeds into.
