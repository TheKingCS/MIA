# Character: your real progression, in one place

## A visual archive of your actual life, not just an avatar

Character pulls together everything the Rewards system already tracks
into one page: your real Level and Prestige, your real lifetime stats
(engine hours, workout hours, missions completed, and more — the same
numbers Skills and Missions already read), your full Collection of
every reward you've unlocked across every challenge chain plus any
hidden achievements you've found, a rarity tally, and your Prestige
emblems. Every number on this page is derived live from real activity
— nothing here is a separate, fabricated score.

## Prestige and rarity

Leveling past 100 starts a new Prestige cycle, each one colored —
white, green, blue, purple, then orange for every cycle after that.
Unlocked rewards carry that same five-tier rarity, from common (white)
up to the rarest (orange); the Collection's rarity tally shows how
many of each you've earned.

## No character art yet

There's no customizable avatar or equip-able cosmetics here yet — a
placeholder stands in for "the character" until real art exists. What
you see is deliberately just the real system underneath: the numbers
and unlocks are genuine, the visual presentation is still to come.
