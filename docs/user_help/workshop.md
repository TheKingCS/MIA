# Workshop & Electronics: Components and the Production Pipeline

## What the Workshop module does

Workshop & Electronics has five tabs: Components, Materials, Jobs,
Products, and Ledger. Components is your electronics parts bin;
the other four together are a small manufacturing pipeline — raw
materials become finished products through jobs, and sales get
recorded against those products.

## Components

A simple parts inventory — resistors, ICs, connectors, whatever you
keep on hand for electronics work. Track name, quantity, and where
something is stored.

## Materials

Raw stock you consume to make things — wood, filament, sheet metal,
whatever a Job draws from. Each Material tracks its own quantity on
hand.

## Jobs

A Job is one production run. It consumes Materials (deducting their
quantity) and, when finished, produces Products (crediting their
quantity) — the real link between what you started with and what you
ended up with, not just two separate lists.

## Products

Finished goods produced by a Job, ready to sell or use. Products track
their own quantity on hand, credited automatically when a Job produces
them.

## Ledger

Revenue and expenses — recording a sale against a Product deducts its
quantity and logs the revenue, so your finances and your inventory
stay in sync automatically rather than needing to be updated by hand
in two places.
