# Home Screen, Power, and Volume

## The Home console's live readouts

Home's left panel shows your Power percentage and plugged-in state plus
Uptime since boot; the Monitoring card in the collapsible right rail
adds Volume, Network, and RAM tiles alongside Power. All of it updates
automatically every few seconds. Your current active Mission and its
progress live in the Mission Log (the Missions tab) now, with a real
Level/XP footer, instead of a card on Home.

## Power

The Power module (and Home's own Power stat/Monitoring tile) show
battery percentage and plugged-in status. You'll get a low-battery
notification automatically if it drops below the configured threshold
while unplugged.

## Energy Sources

The Power module's Energy Sources tab tracks other power sources by
hand — a solar array, a generator, a propane tank, anything you'd
otherwise have to remember on your own. Add Source names a new one;
Log Reading… records a manual reading against it (fuel level, output,
whatever that source tracks); Edit Selected and Delete Selected manage
existing sources.

## Volume

Volume lives in the header now, not on the Home screen — tap your
profile avatar (top-right, a circle with your name's first letter) to
open a menu with a quick volume slider and mute button. It controls the
device's actual system volume. If your device doesn't support system
volume control, it will show as unavailable rather than pretend to do
something it can't.
