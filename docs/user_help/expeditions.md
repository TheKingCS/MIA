# Expedition Mode: Expeditions, Trips, and Memories

## What's the difference between an Expedition and a Trip?

An Expedition is the big container — a dated outing, like "Spring
Backpacking Trip" or "Weekend at the Lake." A Trip is one leg or day
within it — a hike, a paddle, a fishing outing, a bike ride. A short
weekend might have just one Trip; a week-long expedition might have
several, one per day or per activity.

## Planning a route

Inside a Trip you can build a planned route out of your saved
Waypoints, see it plotted on a schematic map, and get the total
planned distance. Add Waypoints first (from the Navigation module, or
just ask the Assistant), then add them to a Trip's route in order.

## Gear checklists

Each Trip has its own gear checklist — pull items straight from your
Inventory or add something custom, and check items off as you pack.

## Trip journal and conditions

Log dated journal entries on a Trip as you go — what happened, and a
separate "conditions" field for weather, since that's useful to look
back on separately from the narrative. Speed and distance you've
actually logged (not just planned) show up here too.

## Photos

Import photos into a Trip to keep a visual record alongside the
journal — they show up later in Memories.

## Memories

Once a Trip or Expedition is done, the Memories module pulls together
a recap: duration, total distance, photo count, and a "View Route Map"
button for each Trip. It's read-only — a look back, not something you
edit directly.
