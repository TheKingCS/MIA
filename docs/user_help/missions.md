# Missions: Goals and Progress Tracking

## What a Mission is

A Mission is a goal you set for yourself — something like "Catch 3
Fish" or "Hike 20 Miles This Month." Missions can stand alone or be
tied to a specific Trip.

## Objectives and progress

Each Mission is made up of one or more Objectives. There are two kinds:

- **Tally objectives** — a manual counter you update yourself, like
  "catch 3 fish." Log progress as you go ("I caught one!") and it adds
  up toward the target.
- **Trip duration objectives** — these compute themselves automatically
  from a linked Trip's logged time splits, so you never have to enter
  anything by hand; the Mission just tracks it live.

## Completing a Mission

Once every Objective on a Mission is met, mark the whole Mission
complete. Completed Missions stay in your list so you can look back on
what you've accomplished.

## The Home screen's Mission card

Your current active Mission and its objective progress show up
automatically on the Home screen, so you always know where you stand
without having to open the Missions module.

## MIA can assign you a Mission

M.I.A. sometimes picks a Mission for you, rather than waiting for you
to create one yourself — this is the gamification side of Missions,
turning real goals into something more like a game's quest log.
MIA-assigned Missions show a gold "MIA ASSIGNED" badge on their card so
you can tell them apart from ones you made yourself. Two rules decide
when this happens: if you've gone a few days with no active Mission,
MIA picks one from a pool of general life-goal templates (things like
decluttering, focused work streaks, or moving around more); if a Task
from the Toolbox's Project Manager sits untouched for several days,
MIA can also turn that specific stale Task into its own one-objective
Mission, so it doesn't just get quietly forgotten.

## Mission cards show overall progress

Each Mission card has its own progress bar showing how many of its
Objectives are complete overall, in addition to each Objective's own
individual progress underneath it.
