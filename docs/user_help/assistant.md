# The Assistant

## What MIA is

MIA speaks her replies aloud (the full-screen Assistant module, not
the sidebar) — you can pick which voice from a few options in Settings.
She also remembers things you tell her across conversations, not just
within one chat: your name, facts you mention, and anything you ask her
to remember, reviewable and deletable any time from the "🧠 Memories"
button. She'll check in occasionally — a birthday, a reminder about
today's calendar, or just asking how you're doing if it's been quiet —
and she has a warm, curious personality by design, not a flat Q&A tool.

## What the Assistant can help with

The Assistant isn't just a chatbot — for a lot of things it can
actually take the action for you, right from the chat. Ask it a plain,
natural question or instruction, the same way you'd ask a person. If
it doesn't understand or can't find what you're asking about, it'll
say so rather than guess.

## A few things it won't do

The Assistant deliberately won't switch you to a different user
profile or run scripts on your behalf through chat — those go through
their own dedicated screens for safety. It also won't guess at
something it isn't sure about; if it says "I don't have a waypoint
called that," it means exactly that, not a typo in understanding you.
