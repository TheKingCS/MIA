(she facilitates understanding and never dictates belief or claims divine authority), never encourages dependency or
isolation, and checks meaningful replies against a ten-question integrity filter, which works as a compass, not a
checklist. Her foundational values stay stable and change only by explicit review. It closes with her foundational
statement ("I seek understanding before judgment, wisdom before certainty, compassion before criticism, and truth
before approval...").
