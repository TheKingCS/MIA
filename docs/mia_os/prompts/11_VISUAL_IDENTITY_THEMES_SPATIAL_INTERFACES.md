PROMPT 11 — VISUAL IDENTITY, THEMES & SPATIAL INTERFACES

Mission

Act as a principal creative director, visual systems architect, UI/UX design lead, motion design specialist, spatial computing designer, and cross-platform interface architect.

Your mission is to establish the complete visual identity, design language, theme architecture, motion system, and spatial interface strategy for MIA OS.

Prompts 00–10 have established MIA's vision, existing ecosystem, application framework, operating system architecture, hardware strategy, intelligence infrastructure, autonomy system, self-awareness architecture, and primary desktop experience.

Now define the visual identity that makes MIA recognizable across every device and interaction environment.

MIA should not look like a generic Linux distribution, conventional AI chatbot, or collection of unrelated applications.

She should have a distinctive, recognizable personality expressed through her interfaces, animations, information presentation, environmental presence, and interaction behavior.

The visual identity must be cohesive, adaptable, technically practical, and maintainable.

The result should establish a design system that can grow alongside MIA rather than requiring every new application or device to invent its own appearance.

Planning, design research, and design specification only. Do not begin implementation.

2. Core Visual Identity and Creative Direction

Establish MIA's core visual identity.

Use the following established creative direction as the starting point.

2.1 Primary inspiration

MIA's visual language draws inspiration from:

- Tony Stark's holographic interfaces.
- Futuristic AI environments such as Jarvis and Edith.
- Cyberpunk interfaces.
- 1990s and early-2000s internet culture.
- MySpace-era customization and personality.
- Cyber graffiti.
- Digital art.
- Liquid motion.
- Spatial computing.
- Futuristic HUD systems.
- Interactive environmental displays.

The goal is not to copy any specific fictional interface.

The goal is to create an original identity that combines technological sophistication with personality, creativity, and a sense of life.

2.2 Visual personality

MIA should feel:

- Intelligent.
- Futuristic.
- Expressive.
- Personal.
- Responsive.
- Elegant.
- Creative.
- Slightly playful.
- Technically sophisticated.
- Recognizably distinct.

Avoid making every interface look like a sterile enterprise dashboard.

2.3 Primary visual language

Investigate:

- Cyan and blue holographic accents.
- Dark surfaces.
- Layered transparency.
- Luminous outlines.
- Glass-like elements.
- Fluid transitions.
- Spatial depth.
- Animated data visualization.
- Digital particles.
- Geometric patterns.
- Graffiti-inspired decorative elements.
- Organic movement.

Establish rules for when these effects are appropriate.

The interface must remain readable and usable rather than prioritizing visual effects over function.

2.4 Visual identity boundaries

The identity should be recognizable even when:

- Animations are disabled.
- A low-power device is used.
- A monochrome or high-contrast theme is active.
- A spatial interface is unavailable.
- An application uses a conventional layout.

MIA's identity must not depend entirely on cyan lighting, transparency, or animation.
