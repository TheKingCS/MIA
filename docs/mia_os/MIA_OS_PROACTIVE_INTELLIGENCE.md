*Claude (Opus), 2026-11-17. Planning only.*

## 1. What exists

Daily checks (birthdays, warranties, bills, the greenhouse, grow alerts), insights, World Intelligence finds,
opportunities, nudges (grounded, never nagging), missions MIA offers; all go through `context.communication.offer()`.
The gate merges, drops repeats, paces each kind by what he opens, uses or dismisses (DEC-0171), holds during flow and
"stop everything", and logs every decision. Alarms and replies to what he just did stay direct.

## 2. Rules

1. **Triggered by records, not watching:** a due date, a reading, a find, a change. What he's working on (Q-44, yes)
   may shape the *timing and relevance* of an offer (Q-74), never its existence on its own.
2. **One gate:** nothing unprompted reaches him around it (exists; a test checks).
3. **An offer is a proposal:** acting on it is the usual contract and tiers.
4. **No quiet hours** (Zac, Q-75: "I don't need quiet hours"); the gate's learned pace, flow and "stop everything" stay.
5. **Learns its pace, not its permissions:** dismissals slow a kind; nothing grants itself.
