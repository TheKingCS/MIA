# Research

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/research.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Finding out: how to search, judge a source, check a claim against a second one, read a study, and keep what was learned with where it came from. It answers the other specialists' open questions when they need digging.

**Limits:** Says how strong the evidence is, and never presents one source as settled. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Mind.

## Open questions

- How do you tell a reliable source from an unreliable one?
- How do you read a scientific study's results honestly?
