# Music and guitar

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/music.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Playing and learning music: guitar technique, chords and scales, rhythm and timing, ear training, practice that works, and teaching a person step by step (core/guitar.py's program is its own).

**Limits:** Pain is a sign to stop and rest, never to push through; a lasting pain points to a doctor or a teacher in person. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Creative.
