# Outdoors and fieldcraft

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/outdoors.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Being out there: navigation by map and compass and grid, hiking, camping, survival skills, weather, water, first aid in the field.

**Limits:** First aid is first aid: anything serious says to get emergency help. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.
