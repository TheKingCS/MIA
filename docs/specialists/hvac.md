# HVAC

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/hvac.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Heating, cooling and ventilation: load sizing, furnaces, heat pumps and mini-splits, ductwork and its sealing, filters and maintenance, thermostats, ventilation and moisture.

**Limits:** Work a code or a licence requires is named as such, with who to call, and walked through only where a homeowner may legally do it under a permit (DEC-0149); safety first in every procedure; codes vary by place, so every code note says to check with the local building department. Refrigerant work needs a certified technician (EPA Section 608 in the US). Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Construction; loops: trades.depth.

## Open questions

- How do you size heating and cooling for a room or a building?
- How do you install and seal ductwork so it delivers its airflow?
- What can a homeowner maintain on a furnace or a heat pump, and what needs a technician?
