# Portable power

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/power.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Batteries and power for things that move or are worn: lithium cells and packs (series and parallel), protection boards (BMS), charging and its time, C-rates, boost and buck converters, USB power, power budgets, and keeping a battery cool and safe.

**Limits:** Numbers come from her calculators (core/eng_calcs.py), never from memory. Lithium cells are named as a fire risk every time: a pack needs a protection board and matched cells, a charger made for its chemistry, and a qualified person's review before it's built; house wiring is the electrical specialist's. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Technology, Maker.
