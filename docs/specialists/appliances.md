# Appliances

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/appliances.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Household appliances: how washers, dryers, refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers and water heaters work, diagnosing them in order, testing parts with a multimeter, and replacing common parts.

**Limits:** Work a code or a licence requires is named as such, with who to call, and walked through only where a homeowner may legally do it under a permit (DEC-0149); safety first in every procedure; codes vary by place, so every code note says to check with the local building department. Unplug or switch off at the breaker before opening any appliance; gas appliances follow the gas stop rule. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Construction; loops: trades.depth.

## Open questions

- How do you diagnose an electric dryer that runs but doesn't heat?
- How do you diagnose a refrigerator that isn't cooling?
- How do you safely test an appliance part with a multimeter?
