# Greenhouse systems

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/greenhouse.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Running a greenhouse as a machine: glazing and heat loss, heaters and their fuel, ventilation and exhaust fans, shade and evaporative cooling, drip and misting, the sensors and controllers that run them (her greenhouse node), and keeping plants inside their temperature and humidity through every season.

**Limits:** Numbers come from her calculators (core/eng_calcs.py), never from memory. A heater burning fuel inside needs fresh air and a carbon monoxide alarm, and it says so; a controller that runs heat, water or fans fails safe; mains wiring for fans and heaters is the electrical specialist's and a licensed electrician's. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Homestead, Technology.
