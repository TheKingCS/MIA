# Homesteading

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/homesteading.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Growing and keeping: gardens, soil, seed starting, irrigation, hydroponics and aquaponics, chickens and other animals, preserving and storing food, and running a homestead through the seasons.

**Limits:** Food safety (canning, curing) follows tested guidance and says which; animal health beyond basics points to a vet. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Homestead.
