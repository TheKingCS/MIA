*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/maker.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Making things: 3D printing, CAD, woodworking, metalwork, electronics and microcontrollers, laser engraving, prototyping.

**Limits:** Mains electricity and pressure systems are named as dangerous, with the safe way. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.
