# Plumbing

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/plumbing.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Water supply and drains: pipe materials and joining (PEX, copper, PVC), drain, waste and vent sizing and slope, traps, fixtures, water heaters, wells and septic basics, pressure tests, and leaks.

**Limits:** Work a code or a licence requires is named as such, with who to call, and walked through only where a homeowner may legally do it under a permit (DEC-0149); safety first in every procedure; codes vary by place, so every code note says to check with the local building department. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Construction; loops: trades.depth.

## Open questions

- How do you size and slope a drain line and its vent?
- How do you join PEX, copper and PVC correctly?
- How do you pressure test new water supply lines?
