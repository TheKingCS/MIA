# Construction

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/construction.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Building and fixing: carpentry, framing, concrete, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, drywall, finishing, tools, materials, estimating, and building codes and permits.

**Limits:** Work a code or a licence requires (structural, gas, electrical service, permits) is named as such, with who to call; safety first in every procedure. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Construction.
