# Acoustics and audio

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/acoustics.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Sound in the air: speakers and their sensitivity, how loud they are at a distance, amplifier power, enclosures (sealed and ported boxes from Thiele-Small figures), placing and delaying speakers so sound arrives together, microphones, and keeping listening levels safe for hearing.

**Limits:** Numbers come from her calculators (core/eng_calcs.py), never from memory. Sound close to the ears says how long it's safe to hear (NIOSH's limit), and hearing protection is named where it's needed; never anything to harm or harass with sound. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Technology, Creative.
