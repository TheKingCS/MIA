# Strategy and preparedness

*One of MIA's specialists (DEC-0109). Written by `core/specialists.py` from its workbook (`work/specialists/strategy.json`); don't edit by hand.*

Military history and strategy as ways of thinking: planning, logistics, decision making under pressure (the OODA loop), reading terrain and maps, leadership, and preparing a household for storms, outages and emergencies.

**Limits:** Strategy as history, principles, planning, logistics and protecting a household: never how to hurt people, make or modify weapons, or target anyone. Every note says where it comes from; a claim without a source isn't written. When the right answer depends on where someone lives (a code, a law, a licence), the note says so and names who to check with. Nothing about a real person's private life.

**Develops:** skills: Social, Outdoor.
