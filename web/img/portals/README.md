# Portal art (H-0096)

The pictures inside the Nexus room's portals (`web/nexus3d.js`), one per area, named by the area's id
(`core/web_areas.AREAS`): `home.jpg`, `garage.jpg`, `plants_animals.jpg`, and so on. Until an area's picture is here,
its portal shows a placeholder in its colour with its icon. Core only offers a picture that exists
(`core/web_nexus.portal_art`). Nothing personal (DEC-0002): no real home, land, faces or names.
